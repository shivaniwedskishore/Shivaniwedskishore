Wedding Itinerary

✦ 🪔 ✦

A sacred schedule of every auspicious moment — so you never miss a beat

🌼   Tuesday, 17th June 2026 — Engagement   🌼
4:00 PM

🙏 Ganapathi Pooja

Seeking the blessings of Lord Ganesha to begin all auspicious proceedings.

5:00 PM

📜 Writing Lagna Pathrika

The sacred wedding invitation scroll is formally written and announced.

6:30 PM

💍 Engagement Ceremony

The formal exchange of rings and blessings between Shivani & Kishore.

8:00 PM

🍽️ Engagement Dinner

A celebratory feast for all guests following the engagement ceremony.

🌿   Tuesday, 24th June 2026 — Wedding Preparations   🌿
5:30 AM

🪔 Preparing Pellikoduku

Rituals begin at the groom's home to prepare Kishore for the wedding ceremonies.

6:00 AM

🙏 Snathakam

The sacred ceremony marking the groom's transition from student to householder.

8:00 AM

🍛 Snathakam Breakfast

A traditional breakfast served following the Snathakam ceremony.

Morning

🌼 Preparing Pellikuthuru

Rituals at the bride's home — Shivani is adorned with turmeric, flowers & blessings.

12:00 PM

🍽️ Snathakam Lunch

A traditional lunch served for family and guests at the groom's residence.

🌙   Tuesday, 24th June 2026 — Pre-Wedding Evening   🌙
6:00 PM

🌅 Yedurukollu

The joyous welcoming of the groom's procession by the bride's family — a cherished Telugu tradition.

7:00 PM

🪔 Vara Pooja

Sacred prayers and rituals performed for the groom by the bride's family.

8:00 PM

🍽️ Dinner

A grand dinner bringing together both families on the eve of the wedding.

9:00 PM

🎶 Mehendi / DJ Night

Celebrate the night away — Mehendi for the ladies and dancing for everyone!

🎊   Wednesday, 25th June 2026 — The Wedding Day   🎊
3:00 AM

🌸 Gowri Pooja

Early morning prayers to Goddess Gowri for blessings upon the bride.

3:00 AM

🙏 Groom Pooja

Sacred rituals performed for the groom in preparation for the wedding.

5:30 AM

🪔 Pre-Wedding Pooja

Auspicious prayers and rituals conducted before the main ceremony begins.

7:00 AM

👰 Bride Entry

Shivani makes her grand, beautiful entrance — the moment everyone has been waiting for.

7:50 AM

⭐ Jeelakarra Bellam ✦ Muhurtham

The most sacred moment — cumin and jaggery are placed on each other's heads, sealing the union of Shivani & Kishore forever.

8:00 AM

🍛 Breakfast

Traditional breakfast served for all guests following the Muhurtham.

10:00 AM

🔥 Wedding Pooja

Sacred fire rituals and Vedic chants performed around the holy Agni.

12:00 PM

🎉 Wedding Events

Talambralu, Saptapadi and other joyful traditional Telugu wedding ceremonies.

1:00 PM

🍽️ Guests Lunch

A grand traditional Telugu feast served on banana leaves for all guests.

2:00 PM

🎊 Wedding Concludes

The beautiful wedding ceremony comes to a joyful close.

4:30 PM

🥹 Appaginthalu

The heartfelt farewell — Shivani begins her beautiful new journey with Kishore.
